Open Disputes

Report content

Anyone can report content stored on HashBin.org. While a report is open, the content is blocked (HTTP 451) until an administrator reviews it. A report is closed when the content is removed, when the claim is denied (the content becomes available again), or after 30 days without a resolution. Reporter contact details are never shown publicly.
