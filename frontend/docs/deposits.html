How deposit amounts are calculated

When you add money to your HashBin balance, you pay three things, and checkout lists each one before you pay:

  1. Balance credit: the amount you chose. All of it is added to your balance.
  2. Card payment cost: what Stripe, our card processor, charges for the payment. HashBin keeps none of it, apart from rounding of at most one cent.
  3. Sales tax, if any applies where you are. Stripe calculates it and shows it at checkout. HashBin doesn't collect sales tax today, so it is $0.00.

The card payment cost, step by step

Stripe charges 2.9% of the whole payment plus $0.30 for a standard US card. Because the 2.9% also applies to the amount added to cover it, the total can't simply be the credit plus 2.9% plus $0.30. Instead:

  1. Add $0.30 to your credit.
  2. Divide by 0.971 (that is, 1 − 0.029), so that 2.9% of the result plus $0.30 leaves exactly your credit.
  3. Round up to the next cent. That is what your card is charged, before any tax.

total = ceiling((credit + $0.30) ÷ 0.971), and the card payment cost is total − credit.

For a $10.00 credit: ($10.00 + $0.30) ÷ 0.971 = $10.6076…, rounded up to $10.61. Stripe takes 2.9% × $10.61 + $0.30 = $0.6077, which it rounds to $0.61, and HashBin receives $10.00, all of which goes to your balance.

The fixed $0.30 matters most on small deposits: it is why a $1.00 credit costs $1.34. Larger deposits lose a smaller share to card costs.

Why we add it instead of building it into prices

Storage is priced close to what it costs us, so a card fee hidden inside it would have to be paid by everyone, including people who deposit once and store for years. Passing the card cost through, itemized and at cost, keeps storage prices low and shows you exactly where the money goes.

Details

  • Other cards: Stripe charges more for some cards, such as international cards or payments needing currency conversion. We always add only the standard US rate above; HashBin pays any difference.
  • Rounding: rounding the total up to a whole cent can leave HashBin with up to one cent more than Stripe's fee. The calculator above shows when that happens.
  • New accounts can add at most $20.00 per checkout during their first 30 days. The minimum deposit is $1.00.
  • Your records: Stripe emails a receipt listing each line. Your transaction history shows the credit, card payment cost, any tax and the total charged for every deposit.
  • Using your balance: storage costs $0.03 per GB per month; see pricing. Deposits are final and balances can't be withdrawn (Terms §3).
  • The code: the server computes the total in grossUp in src/utils/cost-policy.js; this page uses the same formula, and a test checks they agree.
