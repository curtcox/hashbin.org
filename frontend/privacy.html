This policy explains what [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we") collects when you use HashBin.org and 256t.us, why, and who can see it. We collect as little as we can: we don't run advertising or behavioral analytics, and we don't sell personal data.

Anything you upload can be downloaded by anyone who has its content identifier (CID). We don't publish a list of stored content, but CIDs are shared freely and can't be revoked. Don't upload personal information you don't want to be public.
