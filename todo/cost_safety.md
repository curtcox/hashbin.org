# Cost safety: prepaid usage, monitoring and operational protection

## Status

**Repository implementation:** complete for the accepted scope, hardened
2026-10-25. **Production readiness:** blocked on external setup and live
acceptance; no production account work is claimed complete.
**Rows:** L22, S20–S22 in `STATUS.md`; related policies L20/S17.

The goal is to keep variable costs covered by customer payments. Absolute
no-loss operation cannot be guaranteed: public Worker refusals, free inline
traffic, fraud and some singleton funded leases remain billable. The fixed
Workers/domain budget and conservative assumptions are documented in
[the price-floor sheet](../docs/business/price-floor.org).

The accepted implementation and operator runbook are in
[docs/cost-safety.md](../docs/cost-safety.md). This checklist supersedes the
original proposal's delayed counters, 5,000-request minimum, automatic debits
and assumed dashboard availability. Accepted settings use prepaid reservations,
a storage-aware allowance, explicit bandwidth purchases, corrected fee gross-up
with a $1 credit minimum, a $20 checkout cap for accounts under 30 days old,
a ten-year outstanding retention cap, and $25/$50 projection alert/brake defaults.

## Unresolved owner policies

- L20 fee absorption/deposit packs and S17 prepaid-retention shutdown obligations
  need owner/legal decisions before changing customer prices or terms.
- Cold singleton funded leases can exceed the minimum price. Decide whether to
  accept that documented exposure or change the price/lease policy after measuring
  it. The monitor's 50%-of-price warning does not itself remove this exposure.
- If the actual WAF expression or billing notification plan is unavailable,
  choose a supported protection/monitoring arrangement and approve any new fixed
  costs. Those decisions belong in the external checklist before repo follow-up.
